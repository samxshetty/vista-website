Our Story

About VISTA

Versatile Information Science and Technical Aspirants — the Information Science department's own club at NMAMIT.

VISTA started as a simple idea: the Information Science department already had brilliant coders, sharp designers, decent cricketers and even better performers — they just never had one place to bring it all together. So a small group of students set out to build exactly that: a club that didn't force anyone to choose between being technical, creative or competitive.

01

How VISTA was formed

VISTA was founded by a group of Information Science students and faculty who felt the department needed a common ground — somewhere first-years and final-years, coders and performers, could all show up as themselves.

What began as informal meet-ups and one small coding session grew, year on year, into a full-fledged club with its own core team, faculty coordinators and a calendar of events that the rest of the college now looks forward to.

The name itself reflects that intent: VISTA — a wide, open view — built by Versatile Information Science and Technical Aspirants who wanted room to grow in more than one direction.

02

Our purpose

VISTA exists to turn curiosity into practice. We believe growth isn't just technical — it's also about walking on stage for the first time, showing up for a team, or learning to lead a group of people toward one goal.

Every event we run, whether it's a hackathon, a career guidance session, a music night or a sports league, is built around the same idea: give students a low-pressure place to try something new, get better at it, and find people who'll do it alongside them.

  • Build genuine technical skill through hands-on events, not just lectures
  • Give every student, technical or not, a stage to be seen on
  • Prepare final-years for careers with real guidance, not guesswork
03

What we've achieved

Over the years, VISTA has grown from a handful of students into a club of over a thousand members, hosting more than 60 events across tech, culturals and sports.

We've brought home wins at state-level hackathons, placed at inter-college cultural fests like Incridea, and built HackVista into a department hackathon students actively look forward to each year. None of it happened overnight — it's the result of every core team that came before this one choosing to leave the club a little better than they found it.

  • 60+ events hosted across four verticals since founding
  • 1,200+ students who've been part of VISTA
  • 18 awards won across hackathons, culturals and sports
Looking ahead

Our current goal

Right now, VISTA is focused on making career guidance as core to the club as our hackathons and fests already are — pairing students with mentorship, interview prep and industry exposure earlier in their degree. Alongside that, we're working to expand our tech events, deepen ties with alumni, and make sure every student in the department has a genuine reason to walk through VISTA's door at least once.
