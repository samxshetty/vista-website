Built by students,

VISTA is the Information Science department's own club — a space where workshops, hackathons, cultural nights and inter-college sports all live under one roof.

We exist to turn curiosity into practice: whether that's shipping your first project, speaking on a stage for the first time, or just showing up for a team you believe in.

What we do

Everything VISTA runs falls into one of these — pick where you want to jump in.

Career Guidance

Resume reviews, mock interviews, placement prep and mentorship sessions to help you plan what comes next.

Cultural Events

Music nights, open mics and department fests — the fun side of VISTA that brings everyone together.

Sports

Inter-department tournaments across cricket, football and more — competitive, loud, and a lot of fun.

Tech Events

Hackathons, workshops on web dev & AI/ML, project showcases and coding challenges for anyone who wants to build something cool.
